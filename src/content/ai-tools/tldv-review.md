# tl;dv Review (2026)

Every product team eventually hits the same wall. You have recorded forty user interviews over the past month. Each one runs between thirty and sixty minutes. The recordings live in a shared folder somewhere, and the only way to find what a user said about a specific feature is to watch hour after hour of video footage. You know the insight exists. Finding it is the problem.

This is the exact problem tl;dv was designed to solve.

Most AI meeting tools treat the text transcript as the primary deliverable, with the video recording as a secondary backup. tl;dv flips that model entirely. It treats the high-definition video recording as the definitive source of truth and uses its AI-generated text as an interactive navigation layer on top of the raw footage. The name itself makes the product philosophy clear: tl;dv stands for **Too Long; Didn't View** — an acknowledgment that nobody wants to rewatch a forty-five-minute user interview from the beginning just to find a thirty-second moment.

## What Is tl;dv?

**Direct Answer Block:**
**tl;dv is a video-first AI meeting assistant that records full high-definition video of web conference calls, transcribes the audio into text, and generates AI summaries that function as interactive clickable timestamps linked directly to the raw video footage.** Rather than delivering a standalone text document, tl;dv turns each AI-generated summary point into a navigation control: clicking any line in the summary immediately jumps the video player to that exact moment in the recording. Users can clip specific video segments with a single click and compile multiple clips from different recordings into shareable multi-meeting reel collections.

To understand what makes this approach fundamentally different, consider how most meeting tools work. The standard model — used by Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai, and Fathom — treats the transcript as the primary output. The recording exists as a backup if someone wants to verify a specific point, but the transcript is where teams spend their time. tl;dv inverts this. The video is the deliverable, and the text is the interface.

This design decision makes tl;dv highly specialized. For teams whose work depends on capturing human behavior, emotional reactions, visual interface struggles, and conversational nuance — the kinds of signals that disappear entirely in a text transcript — tl;dv provides an entirely different category of value than its competitors.

## Key Features

tl;dv has built a tightly focused feature set around its video-first philosophy. Here is a detailed breakdown of how each core capability works.

![tl;dv dashboard overview showing video-first AI meeting assistant with timestamped summaries and clip library](/images/ai-tools/tldv-review/tldv-dashboard-overview.svg)

### 1. Full High-Definition Video Recording

Unlike tools that record only audio or capture static screenshot images of shared slides, tl;dv records the complete high-definition video stream of your web conference call. Every camera feed, shared screen, and presentation becomes part of the permanent video record.

The recording bot joins your call as a standard participant after connecting your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook. Once the meeting begins, it captures the full visual and audio feed in the background without requiring any active management from the host or participants. The resulting video file is stored in tl;dv's cloud infrastructure and is accessible from your dashboard within minutes of the call ending.

This comprehensive video capture is the foundation that makes every other tl;dv feature possible. You cannot build an interactive video navigation system on top of an audio-only recording.

### 2. Timestamped AI Summaries

After the call ends, tl;dv's AI processes the transcript and generates a structured summary. The critical differentiator is what happens when you interact with that summary. Each line, bullet point, and section header is hyperlinked to the specific timestamp in the video recording where that topic was discussed.

If the AI summary notes that a user mentioned difficulty finding the export button, clicking that line does not take you to a section of the transcript — it starts playing the video from the exact moment the user said it. You can hear the tone of their voice, watch their facial expression, and observe what they were looking at on their screen simultaneously.

This synchronized navigation transforms how teams consume meeting data. Instead of reading through pages of text and then hunting for the relevant video timestamp manually, researchers can navigate a forty-five-minute interview in three minutes by scanning the summary and clicking directly into the moments that matter.

### 3. One-Click Video Clipping

During a live call, tl;dv provides a floating widget that allows you to mark important moments with a single click. When you click the highlight button, tl;dv flags the preceding thirty seconds of audio and video, ensuring the full context of the speaker's statement is captured. You can add a text label to the clip immediately so it is easy to identify later.

After the meeting ends, you can also create clips from the video timeline or the transcript in the post-meeting dashboard. Highlighting a section of text in the transcript and selecting the clip option generates a shareable video segment covering that exact portion of the call. The clip retains synchronized subtitles, making it watchable on mute.

Each clip generates a unique shareable link. Recipients do not need a tl;dv account to view the clip in their browser. This makes it practical to send a clip to an engineering team, a stakeholder, or a client without requiring them to create an account or log into any platform.

### 4. Multi-Meeting Reel Collections

This is one of tl;dv's most powerful and distinctive features. Users can aggregate individual clips from different recordings into a single organized collection called a reel.

The practical application is significant. If you have conducted twelve user interviews and four users each mentioned friction with the onboarding flow, you can clip those four moments — each from a different recording — and combine them into a single reel. The reel plays as a continuous sequence, presenting the evidence from multiple separate users as a cohesive, compelling document. Sharing this reel with leadership or engineering teams condenses hours of video into a precise, multi-source evidence package that takes minutes to watch.

This capability turns individual interview recordings from isolated data points into a structured research deliverable.

![tl;dv meeting workflow showing calendar sync, bot join process, and real-time tagging pipeline](/images/ai-tools/tldv-review/tldv-meeting-workflow.svg)

### 5. Speaker Identification and Tagging

tl;dv automatically identifies distinct speakers in the recording using acoustic diarization. Each speaker's dialogue is labeled separately in the transcript, allowing you to filter the summary or transcript by individual participant.

If you regularly interview users one-on-one, you can quickly filter the transcript to show only the user's responses and none of the moderator's questions. This filtering capability speeds up research review significantly and makes it easy to extract clean quotations for research reports or presentations.

### 6. Multi-Language Transcription

tl;dv supports transcription in over 30 languages. If you conduct international user research across different markets, you can record interviews in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, or Japanese, and tl;dv will transcribe and summarize the conversation accurately.

The cross-language translation feature allows you to conduct an interview in one language and receive the AI summary in another. A user interview conducted in German can generate an English summary automatically, enabling research leads to distribute findings to global teams without requiring manual translation.

### 7. CRM and Workflow Integrations

On paid plans, tl;dv connects to Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, Linear, and Jira. These integrations allow teams to push meeting summaries and clip links directly into project management systems, CRM deal records, or communication channels.

However, this is an area where tl;dv lags significantly behind dedicated sales intelligence tools. The CRM integration is functional but not deep. It does not automatically populate specific CRM fields, track buyer intent across multiple calls, or provide the kind of automated data routing that Fireflies.ai offers on its standard paid plans. Teams that rely heavily on CRM automation for sales workflows will find tl;dv's integration layer too lightweight.

### 8. Team Knowledge Base and Global Search

At the team level, tl;dv builds a searchable repository of all recorded meetings across your organization. Global keyword search allows team members to locate every recording where a specific word or phrase was mentioned across months of stored content. This is particularly valuable for product teams building a longitudinal body of qualitative research over time.

New team members can use the searchable library to quickly get up to speed on historical user feedback for specific product areas without requiring a dedicated onboarding session.

## How Well Does It Work?

Looking past feature descriptions, the real test of a meeting tool is how reliably it performs under the pressures of a normal workday.

### Recording Quality and Reliability

The tl;dv recording bot is consistently reliable for joining scheduled calendar events. When properly configured, it appears in the meeting waiting room on time and captures full video without requiring manual intervention. The video quality in the recording matches the resolution of the original call faithfully. One practical note: if you are not the meeting host, the host must admit the tl;dv bot from the waiting room, which occasionally requires a brief explanation to external participants unfamiliar with AI note-takers.

### Transcription Accuracy

tl;dv's transcription accuracy is strong for standard conversational English and performs well across its supported languages. In quiet environments with adequate microphone quality, the text output requires minimal manual correction. Accuracy can drop when there is significant background noise, heavy cross-talk between multiple speakers, or specialized technical jargon. Paid plan users can upload custom vocabulary lists to improve recognition of brand names and product-specific terminology.

### AI Summary Quality

The AI summaries are useful and well-structured, but they are calibrated for high-level behavioral overviews rather than detailed operational task tracking. A tl;dv summary accurately captures the main themes discussed, user pain points expressed, and broad action items agreed upon. What it does not do well is produce the kind of highly granular, structured corporate meeting notes — with precisely assigned deadlines, numbered action items, and CRM field mappings — that tools like Fireflies.ai generate on their Business tier.

This is a deliberate design choice, not a deficiency. tl;dv is built for research-grade qualitative data, not sales operations efficiency. The summary is designed to guide you to the right video moment, not to replace the need to watch the video.

### Video Clipping Speed

Clip generation is fast. After highlighting a transcript segment and requesting a clip, the shareable link is ready within seconds. The clip quality preserves the original video fidelity, and the synchronized subtitle track is embedded automatically. Sharing the link with a colleague who does not have tl;dv opens a clean, browser-native video player with no account requirements.

### Collaboration and Sharing

Sharing recordings, clips, and reels is straightforward. Permission controls allow team administrators to set recordings as private, team-accessible, or publicly shareable via link. The workspace folder structure is logical and easy to navigate. The global search is fast and surfaces relevant results accurately, even across large libraries of recordings.

## Common Questions

### 1. Is tl;dv free?

tl;dv offers a free plan that provides unlimited recording and transcription. However, the free tier limits AI-generated summaries to 10 lifetime uses and automatically deletes recordings after 3 months. For ongoing research or team use, these restrictions typically make the Pro plan ($18/user/month billed annually) necessary.

### 2. Does tl;dv record video or just audio?

tl;dv records full high-definition video of the web conference call, including all participant camera feeds and shared screen content. This is one of its primary differentiators. Tools like Otter.ai capture only audio and static slide screenshots. tl;dv captures the complete visual record.

### 3. How accurate is tl;dv's transcription?

Based on standard industry use, tl;dv's transcription is highly accurate for conversational English in clear audio environments. It performs well across its supported languages. Accuracy can decrease in noisy environments, with heavy cross-talk, or with highly specialized technical terminology. Paid plan users can add custom vocabulary to improve recognition of product-specific terms.

### 4. Can recipients watch tl;dv clips without a tl;dv account?

Yes. Shareable clip links open in a standard browser video player without requiring the recipient to create an account or log in. This is important for sharing research evidence with engineering teams, stakeholders, or external clients who are not tl;dv users.

### 5. Does tl;dv work if I am not the meeting host?

Yes. The tl;dv bot can join meetings where you are an attendee rather than the host. However, the host must admit the bot from the meeting waiting room. It is good practice to inform the host or mention at the start of the call that you are recording using an AI note-taker, both as a matter of transparency and to comply with applicable privacy laws.

### 6. How does tl;dv handle different languages?

tl;dv supports transcription in over 30 languages including English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, and Japanese. The cross-language translation feature allows you to generate an AI summary in a different language than the one spoken during the meeting. This is particularly useful for distributed research teams operating across multiple markets.

### 7. Is my data secure with tl;dv?

tl;dv encrypts recordings in transit and at rest. The platform complies with GDPR requirements and maintains standard cloud security protocols. For organizations with strict security requirements, review tl;dv's data retention policies and consider whether the Business plan's enterprise security controls meet your IT and compliance standards before deploying company-wide.

### 8. Can I use tl;dv without a bot joining the meeting?

No. Like most cloud-based meeting recorders, tl;dv requires its virtual bot to join the web conference to capture the audio and video stream. If your organization's security policies strictly prohibit external recording bots from entering internal meetings, tl;dv is not a viable solution. Consider local hardware solutions or native platform recording tools instead.
