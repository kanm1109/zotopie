## What Is Fathom?

**Direct Answer:** Fathom is a free AI meeting assistant that automatically joins your video conference calls, records the audio and video, provides a real-time transcript, and generates an organized summary immediately after the call ends. It is designed to replace manual note-taking and streamline the process of sharing meeting outcomes with team members and syncing data to CRM platforms.

Fathom operates as a participant in your online meetings. Once you connect your calendar (Google Calendar or Outlook), the Fathom bot monitors your schedule. When a meeting containing a Zoom, Meet, or Teams link begins, the bot automatically requests entry. During the call, a side panel provides a live transcript and allows you to highlight important moments with a single click. 

After the meeting concludes, Fathom processes the recording within seconds. It delivers a comprehensive dashboard where you can review the full video, read the cleaned-up transcript, and view an AI-generated summary. These summaries can be instantly pushed to other tools in your workspace, such as a Slack channel, a Notion database, or directly into a contact record in HubSpot or Salesforce. The core philosophy behind Fathom is simplicity and speed, aiming to reduce the administrative burden that follows back-to-back virtual meetings.

The platform was built to address the specific pain point of professional context-switching. When you are forced to divide your attention between listening to a client and typing out summaries, the quality of your interaction drops. By handling the documentation mechanically, Fathom allows users to look directly at the camera, maintain eye contact, and participate authentically. The tool acts as an accurate digital memory bank, preserving exactly what was said, who said it, and what next steps were agreed upon, eliminating the ambiguity that frequently follows complex group discussions.

## Key Features

To understand how Fathom performs in a real-world setting, we need to look closely at its core functionality. Below is a detailed breakdown of the features that make Fathom a popular choice, as well as the mechanics of how they operate.

![Fathom dashboard overview showing AI meeting recording, live transcript feed, and real-time highlight controls](/images/ai-tools/fathom-review/fathom-dashboard-overview.svg)

### 1. Unlimited Recording and Transcription
One of the most significant advantages Fathom offers is unlimited recording and transcription on its free plan. While many competitors cap your monthly usage at 300 or 600 minutes, Fathom allows you to record as many meetings as you need. The transcription engine is highly accurate and handles different accents well. It identifies different speakers automatically (speaker diarization) and labels them in the text. This feature alone makes it an invaluable tool for users who spend most of their day on calls and cannot afford restrictive usage limits. 

The underlying engine handles background noise filtering efficiently. Even if a participant is speaking from a noisy environment or using a sub-par microphone, Fathom manages to construct an accurate textual representation of the dialogue. It also strips out filler words like "um," "uh," and "like" automatically in the final transcript, resulting in a cleaner text file that is far easier to skim or read thoroughly.

### 2. Real-Time Highlighting
During a live call, Fathom provides a desktop widget or an integrated panel (depending on the platform) that features highlight buttons. If someone says something important—like mentioning a budget, agreeing to an action item, or providing positive feedback—you simply click a button. Fathom will flag that specific portion of the video and transcript. After the meeting, these highlights are categorized, making it incredibly easy to jump straight to the most important parts of a one-hour call without having to skim through the entire transcript.

This highlighting system is highly customizable on paid tiers. Users can create custom tags that correspond to their specific business practices, such as "Competitor Mentioned," "Technical Objection," or "Follow-up Required." When you click a tag during the call, Fathom marks the preceding 30 seconds of audio, ensuring that the full context of the speaker's statement is captured in that specific highlight clip.

### 3. AI-Generated Summaries
The moment you end a call, Fathom's AI goes to work on the transcript. It does not just provide a giant block of text; it structures the summary based on templates. You can choose templates tailored to specific types of meetings, such as Sales Discovery, Customer Success check-ins, or standard internal team syncs. The AI accurately pulls out action items, assigns them to the correct speaker based on the context of the conversation, and summarizes the main topics discussed. The speed of generation is impressive, typically taking less than a minute even for hour-long meetings.

The summaries use hierarchical formatting, combining bullet points, bolded text, and clear section dividers to ensure maximum readability. For instance, a technical sync summary will clearly separate "Infrastructure Updates" from "Front-End Performance Bugs," list the specific individuals assigned to resolve those bugs, and state the deadlines discussed during the call. This automated structuring removes the human bias and fatigue that often alter manual meeting minutes.

### 4. CRM Integration and Syncing
For sales professionals and account managers, Fathom’s CRM integration is a major time-saver. Available primarily on the paid tiers, Fathom can connect directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, and Close. When a meeting ends, Fathom searches your CRM for the participants' email addresses. It then automatically logs the call, attaches the AI summary, and includes a link to the full recording directly in the contact or deal record. This eliminates the tedious task of manually typing up notes and copying them into your CRM software.

The depth of the integration ensures that specific notes can be routed to different custom fields within your CRM layout. For instance, if your sales team uses a specific methodology like MEDDPICC or BANT, Fathom's premium AI structures can identify answers to questions regarding budget or decision-making timelines and map those specific text blocks into the corresponding CRM fields, ensuring clean data hygiene across the entire sales org.

### 5. Multi-Language Support
Fathom is not restricted to English. According to information available at publication time, it supports over 28 languages. If you conduct business internationally, Fathom can accurately transcribe languages like Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Portuguese. Furthermore, you can speak in one language and have the system generate the post-meeting summary in another. For example, you can conduct a meeting in German, but have the Fathom AI generate the bulleted summary and action items in English to share with your global team.

This cross-lingual capability is particularly beneficial for distributed software teams or multinational agencies. It reduces misunderstandings caused by language barriers, as team members can review the text in their preferred language alongside the original audio file. The engine also handles bilingual meetings smoothly, tracking speaker changes even when participants switch between languages mid-sentence.

### 6. Video Clipping and Sharing
Sometimes sharing a full 45-minute recording is too much. Fathom makes it easy to create bite-sized clips. By highlighting a section of the transcript, you can instantly generate a shareable link to just that specific video segment. This is incredibly useful for sharing product feedback directly with an engineering team, or sharing a positive customer testimonial with the marketing department. The recipient does not need a Fathom account to view the clip.

The video clips retain the synchronized subtitle track, making them easy to watch even on mute. Additionally, users can string multiple individual clips together into a single "playlist." For example, a user could compile five different clips of different customers complaining about a specific software bug and send that curated playlist to the product manager as evidence for why a particular feature needs immediate attention.

### 7. Team Folders and Organization
If you use Fathom within a company, the Team Edition allows you to create centralized folders. You can set up folders for specific projects, departments, or clients. Team members can automatically route their meeting recordings into these shared spaces. Furthermore, Fathom includes a global search function. If you need to remember what was said about "Project Apollo" three months ago, you can search that keyword, and Fathom will pull up every transcript across your team's folders where that phrase was mentioned.

This centralized repository acts as a highly searchable knowledge base for the entire organization. New employees can be added to a folder and quickly get up to speed on historical client conversations or internal engineering design discussions without requiring hours of manual onboarding or debriefing meetings from senior staff members.

### 8. Strict Privacy and Security Measures
In the current software environment, inputting confidential business conversations into AI tools raises valid security concerns. Fathom addresses this directly by maintaining SOC 2 Type II compliance. More importantly, Fathom explicitly states that they do not sell user data and they do not use your private meeting transcripts to train their foundational AI models. This commitment to privacy makes Fathom much easier to clear through IT and security reviews compared to some newer, less established AI tools.

Encryption standards are strictly maintained both in transit and at rest. Video recordings are securely stored on AWS servers using AES-256 encryption, and all communication between your browser, video platforms, and Fathom's servers uses encrypted TLS 1.3 channels. This technical setup ensures that proprietary business discussions, financial projections, and sensitive customer data remain fully protected from unauthorized access.

## How Well Does It Work?

Features on a pricing page are one thing, but everyday usability is another. Based on extensive testing and user feedback, here is how Fathom performs across the three stages of a meeting: setup, execution, and post-meeting wrap-up.

### The Setup Process
Getting started with Fathom is remarkably frictionless. You sign up using your Google or Microsoft account, which automatically links your calendar. Fathom then prompts you to install a browser extension or a desktop application. The interface is clean, relying on standard design patterns that require no learning curve. You can configure your joining preferences immediately: you can set Fathom to join every single calendar event that has a video link, only meetings with external participants, or only meetings you manually select. This level of granular control prevents the bot from accidentally joining sensitive internal 1-on-1s unless you want it there.

![Fathom meeting workflow showing calendar sync, bot join process, and real-time transcription pipeline](/images/ai-tools/fathom-review/fathom-meeting-workflow.svg)

The calendar syncing logic is robust. It scans for changes continuously, meaning if a meeting is rescheduled or canceled at the last minute, Fathom updates its queue within seconds. This prevents ghost bots from entering abandoned meeting rooms or missing meetings that were shifted earlier in the day. The setup dashboard also allows you to whitelist specific domains, ensuring that calls with family members or medical professionals remain completely private and ignored by the software.

### During the Meeting
When the meeting starts, Fathom appears as a participant (e.g., "Fathom Notetaker"). It operates quietly in the background. If you are using Zoom, Fathom integrates directly into the Zoom interface via an app, which feels native and unobtrusive. For Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, it utilizes a floating desktop widget. The real-time transcription is responsive, with words appearing on the screen with only a minor, acceptable delay. 

The highlighting feature during the call is intuitive. If you forget to click a highlight, you do not need to panic; you can easily go back and highlight sections in the transcript after the call. The only minor friction point is that participants must admit the Fathom bot from the waiting room. If you are not the host of the meeting, you will need to ask the host to let your Fathom bot in. This requirement can occasionally lead to a brief explanation at the start of a call if the other party is unfamiliar with AI note-takers.

### After the Meeting
This is where Fathom truly shines. The post-meeting processing is exceptionally fast. By the time you have closed your video conferencing app and opened your browser, Fathom has usually finished compiling the video, transcript, and AI summary. 

The accuracy of the summaries is high. It rarely hallucinates or invents conversation points. It is particularly good at identifying action items and noting who is responsible for them. Sharing these notes takes one click. You can copy a formatted text block to paste into an email, or use the native integrations to push the notes to a Slack channel. The entire workflow—from call end to notes shared—takes less than two minutes, representing a massive reduction in administrative work.

The post-meeting dashboard also includes a text editor, allowing you to manually polish the summary before exporting it. If the AI slightly misunderstood a specific acronym or technical product name unique to your business, you can quickly fix it in the dashboard. Any changes you make in the editor are preserved when you sync the text block over to Slack, Notion, or your preferred CRM platform.

## Common Questions

### Does Fathom work if I am not the host of the meeting?
Yes. You can use Fathom even if you are just an attendee. However, the Fathom bot will enter the meeting's waiting room (if one is enabled), and the host will need to admit the bot. You may also need to inform the host that you are recording the call, depending on local privacy laws. Many users find it helpful to add a note to their calendar invite or mention at the start of the call that they are using an AI note-taker to ensure transparency and comfort for all participants.

### Is Fathom truly free forever?
According to information available at publication time, Fathom has committed to keeping its core features—recording, transcription, and basic summaries—completely free without usage caps. They monetize the platform by charging for premium integrations, team features, custom summary templates, and advanced AI systems. This freemium model ensures that individual users can rely on the basic product without fear of hitting sudden paywalls or minute restrictions.

### How does Fathom handle different languages?
Fathom supports over 28 languages. You can set your primary language in the application settings. If you join a meeting where a supported language is spoken, Fathom will transcribe it accurately. You can also prompt the AI to translate the final summary into a different language. For example, if your team holds a meeting in Spanish but needs to distribute the action items to English-speaking stakeholders, Fathom handles this translation layer seamlessly during post-meeting compilation.

### Is my meeting data safe with Fathom?
Fathom takes privacy very seriously. They are SOC 2 Type II compliant. Most importantly, their privacy policy clearly states that they do not sell your data, nor do they use your private meeting recordings or transcripts to train their AI models or any third-party foundational models. All files are encrypted at rest and in transit, and access controls within the software ensure that only designated users within your organization can view specific meeting recordings or data entries.

### Can I stop Fathom from recording midway through a call?
Yes. You maintain full control over the Fathom bot during the meeting. You can pause the recording at any time using the desktop widget or Zoom app interface if the conversation shifts to sensitive, personal, or confidential topics. You can also permanently delete the entire recording and transcript from your Fathom dashboard immediately after the call ends, ensuring that nothing is saved on Fathom's servers if a meeting needs to be entirely off the record.
