## False Positive Analysis: The Commercial Risk Balancing Act

A false positive occurs when an AI detector flags purely human text as machine-written. This metric represents the highest source of friction for individual copywriters and editors. If a classification system is tuned to be overly aggressive, it can result in incorrect accusations, damaging professional relationships with trusted freelance networks.

### The Trade-Off of Sensitivity
Originality.ai makes no secret of its aggressive positioning. Because its target customer is an enterprise publisher or content buyer who cannot afford to let synthetic text slip into their index, the software’s underlying neural networks are optimized to prioritize minimizing false negatives (missing actual AI text) over minimizing false positives. It treats detection like a highly sensitive filter; it prefers to flag borderline text for editorial review rather than clear a document that might contain machine generation.

### High-Risk Content Segments
Our extensive verification runs confirmed that this aggressive tuning can create notable friction when parsing specific styles of human writing:
*   **Highly Formal or Academic Prose:** Human text that relies heavily on structured transition formulas (e.g., *In conclusion*, *Furthermore*, *It is important to note*) and balanced, uniform sentence lengths will frequently trigger elevated AI scores on Originality.ai.
*   **Technical and Legal Documentation:** Guides that explain granular software installation sequences or list regulatory safety mandates naturally display low variance in pattern changes (low burstiness). Because the human writer must write with complete precision, the resulting text mathematically mirrors an AI model, causing Originality.ai to flag the work.
*   **Non-Native English Writing:** Authors writing in English as a second language frequently use highly standard grammar patterns, conventional vocabulary pairs, and predictable transitions to protect their text from errors. Originality.ai's models can interpret this lack of idiomatic chaos as a synthetic signature, requiring editors to manually verify the content before taking disciplinary action.
