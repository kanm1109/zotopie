Pricing checked: 2026-11-24

If you sell courses or coaching through a link in bio, the tool you pick takes a cut of every sale — and the cheapest option depends on how much you sell per month. If you sell under ~$322/month, Beacons Free keeps the most of your sale: $0 plan fee and a 9% seller fee. Above ~$322/month, Stan Store Creator ($29/month, 0% seller fee) keeps the most, beating Beacons Creator Plus ($30/month, 0%) by $1/month — on fees alone the two are effectively tied. Fee-only cross-check: Linktree Pro ($17/month, 0%) is cheaper than either on plan cost alone — see the [hub](/best/link-in-bio-tools/).

## The math (show your work)

Platform cost = plan fee + seller fee. Payment processing (Stripe 2.9% + $0.30/order) is the same on both, so it does not change the ranking — but it is included in the "keeps" figures below, exactly as our [fee calculator](/tools/link-in-bio-fee-calculator/) does it.

- **Beacons Free vs Stan Creator:** $0 + 9% of sales vs $29 + 0%. Break-even: 0.09 × S = 29 → S = **$322.22/month ≈ $322**. Below $322, Beacons Free is cheaper; above $322, Stan Creator is cheaper.
- **Beacons Free vs Beacons Creator Plus ($30/month, 0%):** break-even at $333.33/month.
- **Beacons Creator ($10/month, 9%) vs Creator Plus ($30/month, 0%):** break-even at $222.22/month.
- **Stan Creator vs Beacons Creator Plus:** same 0% seller fee, so Stan wins by exactly $1/month at every sales level ($29 vs $30).

## FAQ

**Does Stan Store have a free plan?**
No. There is a 14-day free trial, then the cheapest plan is Creator at $29/month with 0% seller fees.

**Which is cheaper for a coach just starting out?**
Under ~$322/month in sales, Beacons Free — it costs nothing per month and takes 9% per sale. Above ~$322/month, Stan Creator's flat $29/month costs less than 9% of your sales.

**Can I earn commission recommending these tools?**
Beacons pays 25% recurring commission for the lifetime of each referred creator, and joining is free. Stan Store pays 20% lifetime, but you must keep a paid $29/month plan to receive commissions.
