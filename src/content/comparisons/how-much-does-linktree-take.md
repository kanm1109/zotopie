# How Much Does Linktree Take From a Sale? (2026)

## Short answer

It depends on your plan. Linktree Free takes 0% — because it cannot sell paid products at all. Starter takes **9% of every sale plus $9/month**. Pro and Premium take **0% of sales** — you pay only the plan fee ($17/month Pro, $35/month Premium) plus payment processing. On every plan, card payments also cost Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 per order, which is not Linktree's fee but comes out of the same sale.

## When does Pro become cheaper than Starter?

Starter costs $9/month + 9% of sales; Pro costs $17/month + 0%. Break-even: 0.09 × S = 8 → S = **$88.89/month ≈ $89**. Sell more than ~$89/month and Pro takes less in total than Starter. On annual billing (Starter $7, Pro $14), the break-even is lower: 0.09 × S = 7 → S = **$77.78/month ≈ $78**.

On fees alone, Premium never beats Pro: both charge 0% per sale and Pro's plan fee is $18/month lower.

## FAQ

**Does Linktree Free take a cut of sales?**

No — but only because Free cannot sell paid products. To sell anything you need Starter or higher.

**Does Linktree Pro take a percentage?**

No seller fee on Pro or Premium. You still pay the $17 (or $35) plan fee and Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 per order.

**What about annual billing?**

$7/month Starter, $14/month Pro, $30/month Premium when billed yearly. The 9% / 0% seller fees are the same.
