# Linktree vs Beacons for Etsy Sellers (2026)

Pricing checked: 2026-11-24

If you sell on Etsy and send buyers to your shop through a link in bio, the tool you pick takes a cut of every sale — and the cheapest option depends on how much you sell per month. If you sell under ~$189/month, Beacons Free keeps the most of your sale: $0 plan fee and the same 9% seller fee as Linktree's cheapest selling tier. Above ~$189/month, Linktree Pro ($17/month, 0% seller fee) keeps the most. Linktree Free cannot sell paid products at all, so it is not an option for Etsy sellers.

## The math (show your work)

Platform cost = plan fee + seller fee. Payment processing (Stripe 2.9% + $0.30/order) is the same on both, so it does not change the ranking — but it is included in the "keeps" figures below, exactly as our [fee calculator](/tools/link-in-bio-fee-calculator/) does it.

- **Beacons Free vs Linktree Pro:** $0 + 9% of sales vs $17 + 0%. Break-even: 0.09 × S = 17 → S = **$188.89/month ≈ $189**. Below $189, Beacons Free is cheaper; above $189, Linktree Pro is cheaper.
- **Linktree Starter vs Linktree Pro:** $9 + 9% vs $17 + 0%. Break-even: 0.09 × S = 8 → S = **$88.89/month ≈ $89**. Above ~$89/month, Pro beats Starter.
- **Linktree Starter vs Beacons Free:** same 9% seller fee, but Starter adds a $9/month plan fee — Beacons Free is **$9/month cheaper at every sales level**.
- **Beacons Free vs Beacons Creator Plus ($30/month, 0%):** break-even at $333.33/month.

## Fee comparison

| | Linktree | Beacons |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | $0 — cannot sell paid products | $0 — sells at 9% seller fee |
| Paid plans / month (monthly billing) | Starter $9; Pro $17; Premium $35 (annual: $7 / $14 / $30) | Creator $10; Creator Plus $30; Creator Max $100 |
| Seller fee (per sale) | Starter 9%; Pro 0%; Premium 0% | Free 9%; Creator 9%; Creator Plus 0%; Creator Max 0% |

Beacons also includes email sends on its plans: Free includes 50 sends/month, Creator includes 500 sends/month. Linktree raised its prices in October 2026 — older figures of $5/$9/$24 per month are outdated and no longer apply.

## FAQ

**Does Linktree take a % of my sales?**
Starter takes 9% per sale on top of the $9/month plan. Pro and Premium take 0% — you pay only the plan fee plus payment processing. Free takes 0% because it cannot sell paid products.

**Is Beacons Free really free?**
The plan is $0/month, but every sale costs 9%. At $500/month in sales that is $45 — still cheaper than any paid plan until you pass ~$333/month (Creator Plus) or ~$189/month (Linktree Pro).

**Which is cheaper at my sales volume?**
Under ~$189/month: Beacons Free. Above ~$189/month: Linktree Pro. Use the calculator above with your own numbers.

**Can I earn commission recommending these tools?**
Linktree has no affiliate program. Beacons pays 25% recurring commission for the lifetime of each referred creator, and joining is free.
