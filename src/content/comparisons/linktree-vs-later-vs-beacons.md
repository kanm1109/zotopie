## FAQ

**Which is cheapest if I sell nothing yet?**
Beacons Free — $0/month, and you only pay 9% when a sale actually happens. Linktree Free costs $0 too, but it cannot sell paid products. Later has no free plan listed on its pricing page; its Starter plan is $25/month from the first day.

**Which is cheapest at $1,000/month?**
Linktree Pro: $17/month platform cost, and it keeps $942.00 of $1,000 in $25-order sales after processing. Beacons Free would cost $90.00 (9%); Later Starter $25.00.

**Does Later take a percentage of my sales?**
No seller fee is published on Later's official pricing page. Later charges a flat subscription; the sale itself happens on your store, which charges its own fees.

**Can I earn commission recommending these tools?**
Linktree has no affiliate program. Beacons pays 25% recurring commission for the lifetime of each referred creator, free to join.
